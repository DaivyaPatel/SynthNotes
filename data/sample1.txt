Machine Learning has transformed the way organizations analyze information and solve real-world problems by enabling systems to continuously improve through experience. Unlike traditional programming, ML models adapt their behavior based on training data, allowing them to recognize images, understand speech, detect anomalies, and forecast future trends. Popular algorithms include Decision Trees, Random Forests, Support Vector Machines, and Neural Networks, each offering unique strengths depending on the application. Despite its advantages, machine learning faces challenges such as data quality, model bias, interpretability, and computational requirements. As research advances, emerging areas like explainable AI, federated learning, and edge machine learning are making intelligent systems more transparent, secure, and efficient.
